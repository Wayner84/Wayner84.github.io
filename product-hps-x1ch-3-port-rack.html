HP-S-X1C/H 3 port rack

Three-port probe rack for HP-S-X1C/H compatible probe holders. Designed to work with existing original rack setups or as a separate additional rack.

PriceEnquireNot ready for sale yet
Enquire

What it is

A 3 port rack for managing HP-S-X1C/H compatible probe holders. Three ports is the standard layout, with more or fewer ports available as a custom machined option on enquiry.

Materials and construction

  • Solid aluminium body.
  • Steel pins for locating the probe holders.
  • ABS probe rest to minimise wear during probe changing operations.

Installation and use

The rack is compatible with existing original rack setups, but can also be installed separately for additional probe management. This product is nearly finished but is not currently listed for sale.
