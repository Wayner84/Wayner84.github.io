Metrology & engineering IT support

Practical help for CMM and engineering environments where normal IT support does not always understand the software, hardware or inspection workflow.

What we can help with

Workstations & upgrades

Replacement PCs, SSD/RAM/GPU upgrades, stability tuning and hardware recommendations matched to the software and workload.

Metrology software environments

Support around PC-DMIS, MCOSMOS, QCT and related software environments: setup, updates, configuration and sensible troubleshooting.

Troubleshooting

Crashes, slow performance, driver issues, storage problems and awkward hardware faults explained with clear next steps.

Point cloud workflows

Help importing, organising and handling scan or point cloud data so it is usable in inspection and engineering contexts.

Remote or local

  • Remote support: setup, diagnosis, guidance and review.
  • Local support: Gloucester-area hardware swaps, builds and hands-on troubleshooting by arrangement.

Useful information to send

  • Software name/version and what you are trying to do
  • PC specs: CPU, RAM, GPU, storage and Windows version
  • Symptoms, error messages, screenshots or logs
  • Whether the job can be remote or needs local support
