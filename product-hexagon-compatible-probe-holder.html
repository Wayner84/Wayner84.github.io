What it is

A compatible replacement-style probe holder for HP-S-X1C/H and DST 200 probe changing setups, designed for one M3 stylus.

Materials and construction

  • Made entirely from durable resin.
  • Fitted with a neodymium magnet with similar clamping force to an original holder.
  • Durable in normal use, but resin location features are not abrasion-proof.

Use and calibration

These holders have been tested extensively, but operator care is still required. Always check calibration results before use and confirm calibration remains within your company parameters.
