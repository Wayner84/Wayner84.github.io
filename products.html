Custom fixturing & workholding

If the standard products are close but not quite right, custom fixturing can be designed around the part, the process, and what you need the setup to achieve.

  • Custom nests and supports

  • Locating features and alignment aids
  • Part-specific workholding solutions

To quote quickly

  • STEP file (preferred) or STL + key dimensions
  • What you're trying to achieve (assemble / inspect / hold / align)
  • Any “must not touch” surfaces
  • Quantity and deadline
  • Whether a standard product is close, or whether you need a fully custom solution
