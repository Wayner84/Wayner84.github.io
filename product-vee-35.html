Standard product

35 × 35 × 50 mm Vee Block

Compact standard vee block for repeatable support and alignment of round stock, tube, shafts and cylindrical parts during inspection and workshop setups.

Price£80.00
Request a quote

What it is for

Useful for inspection setups, part support, repeatable alignment, and general workshop fixturing where a compact standard vee is enough.

Materials

Resin vee block with machine-finished working faces. Clamp components use ABS, brass and steel hardware where supplied.

Typical uses

  • Supporting round parts for inspection
  • Quick alignment in fixtures
  • Repeatable bench or workshop setups
  • General-purpose cylindrical part location

Accuracy

  • Linear dimensions typically within ±0.25 mm.
  • Vee angle within ±0.2° of 90°.
  • Flatness of 30 microns and under in most cases.
