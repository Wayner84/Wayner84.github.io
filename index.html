How it works

1) Send files

STEP preferred. STL is fine for print-only. A sketch with key dimensions works for early-stage quotes.

2) We review like engineers

We’ll suggest materials/process, highlight risk areas (warp, supports, tolerances), and propose simple design tweaks if needed.

3) Clear quote + lead time

You’ll get options, lead time, and a clear quote. If you’ve got a deadline, tell us and we’ll confirm feasibility.
