Capabilities

Practical design, printing, finishing and support for small engineering teams, workshops and inspection environments.

Design & engineering

  • CAD modelling for parts, assemblies and fixtures
  • Design-for-additive review for strength, printability and distortion risk
  • Fit, tolerance and datum guidance
  • Iteration support from rough idea to usable part

3D printing & finishing

  • FDM/FFF printing for prototypes, tooling and robust utility parts
  • Resin/MSLA printing for detailed parts, fit checks and small fixtures
  • Basic post-processing, deburring and clean-up
  • Machine finishing where the job needs more controlled working surfaces

Fixturing & workholding

  • Vee blocks, soft jaws, nests and locating aids
  • Inspection supports and repeatable setup aids
  • Assembly jigs, simple poka-yoke ideas and alignment features
  • Custom clamps and ergonomic workholding details

Materials

  • FDM: ABS, PLA, PETG, TPU, PVA and speciality PLA options
  • Resin: Elegoo ABS-like 2.0 / 3.0 / 3.0+ and 8K Standard
  • Colours and exact material choice confirmed at quote stage

Metrology & engineering IT

Alongside physical products, we can help with metrology-minded IT: workstation upgrades, troubleshooting, PC-DMIS / MCOSMOS / QCT environments, and point cloud workflow support.
